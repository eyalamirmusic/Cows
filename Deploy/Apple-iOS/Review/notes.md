# Notes for App Review (Cows In Love, iOS and macOS)

Attached: a screen recording from an iPhone 12 mini on the current iOS, from
launch through the typical flow (gameplay-iphone12mini.mp4, 53 s).

1. Screen recording: attached. The app has no account registration, login,
   account deletion, user-generated content, or paid content, so none of
   those flows exist to record.

2. Purpose and audience: Cows In Love is a small single-player 3D game. A cow
   walks through a misty meadow looking for the other cow; the player moos to
   hear where she is, hops fences and logs, crosses a plank bridge, and finds
   her. It is for anyone who wants a gentle five-minute game; rated 4+. The
   value is play, nothing else.

3. Setup and access: none needed. Launch the app and the game starts. Walk
   with the on-screen stick (bottom left), Moo and Jump with the two buttons
   (bottom right), drag anywhere to look. Finding the other cow ends the
   round and the next meadow begins. No credentials, no sample files, no
   settings.

4. External services: none. The app makes no network requests, uses no
   analytics, advertising, authentication, payment or AI services, and
   collects no data. Everything runs on the device.

5. Regional differences: none. The app is identical in every region and
   language (English only).

6. Regulated industry or third-party material: none. All art, audio and code
   are our own.
